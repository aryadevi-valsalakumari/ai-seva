Sample use cases

What this looks like in practice

Service business

Calls missed after hours

Root cause: nobody is available to answer outside office time.

Solution: a 24/7 AI voice agent that answers, qualifies callers and books appointments.

Sales team

Root cause: customer data lives in many places with no single view.

Solution: a custom AI CRM with a live dashboard and automatic follow-up reminders.

Root cause: data is spread across tools and joined manually.

Solution: automated business intelligence that updates itself and highlights what needs attention.
