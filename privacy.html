Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how Ai-SEVA (Artificial Intelligence for Social Empowerment Vision Associates, Kozhikode, Kerala, India) handles information when you use this website, ai-seva.sucselix.com.

1. Information we receive

  • What you send us. If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, business name, phone number, email address and your message.
  • Contact form. The form on this site does not send data to our servers. It opens WhatsApp with your message ready, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may automatically record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, to keep the site secure and working.
  • Demo assistant. The demo assistant on the home page runs in your browser using prepared answers. What you type into it is not sent to us or stored.

2. Cookies and tracking

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies and does not currently use analytics tools. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a free AI audit.
  • To prepare quotes and deliver services you ask for.
  • To keep this website secure and working.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

We use third-party services to run the website and communicate with you, including Vercel (hosting), GitHub (code storage), Meta WhatsApp and Google Gmail. They process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry messages only as long as needed to respond, provide services and meet legal or accounting requirements, then delete or anonymise them.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you may withdraw consent for us to contact you. Write to server.iseva@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time, in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending passwords, bank details or other highly sensitive data in an enquiry.

This website is meant for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
